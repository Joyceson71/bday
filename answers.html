🔗 Live Google Sheet Real-Time Sync

To receive every answer directly in your Google Sheet the moment Amma types or dictates it:

  1. Open sheets.new to create a Google Sheet.
  2. Click Extensions > Apps Script and paste the code from scripts/google_apps_script.js.
  3. Click Deploy > New deployment > Web app (Set 'Who has access' to 'Anyone').
  4. Paste your Web App URL into js/config.js under sheetsWebhookUrl.
